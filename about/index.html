01ABOUT US

Your trusted partner

Mecca Auto & General specialises in genuine parts and repair services for Toyota, Isuzu, Ford and Nissan vehicles.

With a steadfast commitment to quality, safety and environmental sustainability, we offer a comprehensive range of products and services designed to meet the diverse needs of our clientele.

We recognise that each vehicle and owner have unique requirements. Our team provides personalised attention and tailored solutions that enhance performance and reliability.

Outstanding customer service remains at the core of our operations. We believe that building lasting relationships with our customers is essential to our success.

Our unique blend of technical expertise, efficient service and reliable spare parts makes us a partner for individual vehicle owners and organisations.
